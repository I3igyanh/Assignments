Object-fit Comparison

All the three boxes below have the same dimension and are the same image

cover

Fills the box completely. Image is cropped if needed. Aspect ratio is preserved.

contain

The whole image fits inside the box. Gaps appear on the sides or top/bottom. Aspect ratio is preserved.

fill

Stretches the image to fill the box exactly. Aspect ratio is not preserved — image distorts.
