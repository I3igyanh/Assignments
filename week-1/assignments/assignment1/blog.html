Why is video tag placed inside article tag?

video tag element is embedded directly inside the article tag because it is content-specific — it illustrates and extends the article's argument about semantic HTML. A video placed in a header tag or aside tag would imply it belongs to the whole site or is supplementary material. Here, the video is part of the article's editorial body, making article tag the correct semantic parent.

Why is audio tag placed inside article tag?

The audio tag clip is a companion to this specific article — a podcast recording that covers the same topic. Because its meaning is inseparable from the article's context, it belongs inside article tag rather than in a global header tag. If a user shares just this article, the audio should travel with it. Placing media inside article tag preserves that relationship and correctly signals to crawlers and assistive technology that the media is editorial content, not decorative or navigational.
